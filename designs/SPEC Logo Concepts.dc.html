Logo concept · the claw is the hand

It goes round once, snaps, and stays

The O is the outcome and the clock. The claw is the hand: it pivots at the centre, travels with the score through red and amber, and when the ring closes at green it lands on twelve and snaps. Then it stays there. It never runs back to red, because the point of the mark is starting something and finishing it — and a business that has finished does not go back to the beginning.

What the motion says

It starts at twelve, red and closed up. Nothing has been proved yet.

It travels. The hand moves because the score moves, not because time passes — a business that stalls has a hand that stalls.

It overshoots twelve by a few degrees and comes back. That is the snap, and it is the only moment of aggression in the whole mark.

Then it stays. A closed green ring with the hand resting on twelve is the brand at rest, and the version used everywhere outside the product. It plays once and holds — the loop is the one thing this mark must never do.

Where it earns its keep

The same object at every scale: the favicon, the nav brand, a role's month, the business score, the board pack cover. One idea rather than a logo and a separate set of charts.

For the designer's brief

The claw pivots at the centre and points outward. Keep the blades soft — curved, rounded tips, no spikes — and keep the pivot body chunky enough to read at 16px.

Hand angle equals the score: twelve o'clock is both zero and one hundred, which is the point. It travels a full turn to get back to where it started, having proved something.

Fixed colour states, no gradient between them: red below 75, amber 75 to 89, green at 90. A gradient would let a business sit visually between pass and fail.

The snap is a slight overshoot and settle, around 8 degrees, no more. It plays once and holds on twelve, and it never loops or resets to red — anywhere.

Deliver SVG with the ring as one stroked circle and the hand as one rotatable group, so a developer can drive both from a score. Plus a static master: green ring, hand on twelve.
