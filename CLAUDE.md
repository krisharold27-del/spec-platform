# SPEC Platform — rule book for every Claude Code session

You are building the SPEC platform for SPEC Business Solutions. Read this file first in every session. It is the product's institutional memory; `DECISIONS.md` records every correction made along the way. The full specification is `docs/SPEC_Master_Requirements.md` and the phased plan is `docs/SPEC_Platform_Build_Plan.md`.

## What SPEC is

SPEC — Safety, People, Earnings, Compliance — is a GM-to-board operating system. Guiding principles, in order: make money; do it safely; ensure all staff love their job and are amazing at it. Compliance and governance protect those three; they are not a competing priority.

The client self-diagnoses with four yes/no questions (are we going well in Safety? People? Earnings? Compliance?), the system isolates the hurting pillar, drills to the specific cause, and prescribes the fix for that pillar only.

## Non-negotiable design rules

1. **Role first, person second.** Roles are defined by what the business needs; a person is then assigned to a role. Criteria and KPIs hang off `roles`, never off `users`. A role may exist with nobody in it; a user cannot exist without a role. Reassigning a person never edits the role.
2. **Claude is the interface, not a feature.** Onboarding, training, "what next", "why this KPI" are all conversational and reason from these principles — never "because the consultant said so".
3. **If it's not simple, it's wrong.** Prefer fewer screens, fewer fields, plain words.
4. **Solutions, not commentary.** Any report of a miss carries a proposed fix.
5. **The chassis transfers; only questions and targets are per-client.** Nothing client-specific in the repo — no names, no financials. Clients live only in their own tenant rows.
6. **Nothing lives only in a chat.** Decisions go in `DECISIONS.md`; specs in `docs/`.

## Scoring (implemented in `src/lib/scoring.ts`, tested against the Master Scorecard)

- Weighted Y / N / NA criteria per role per pillar. `pillar = Σ(weight where Y) / Σ(weight where ≠ NA)`. Blank = not achieved, still in denominator (an empty template scores 0, not error).
- Person = mean of four pillars. Team pillar = mean across roles.
- Weights within a pillar must sum to 100%; the UI enforces this on save (`validateWeights`). Source sheets did not always do so.
- Two KPIs per pillar per role, negotiated with the role holder. Safety KPIs mirror across roles (zero incidents, zero workers comp claims).
- **90% rule:** the business "is SPEC" only when all four team pillars are ≥ 90% for two consecutive months.
- **Hard gates**, pass/fail, reported separately from scores: Zero Harm (any LTI/MTI/psychosocial > 0 fails); Clear to Work (training compliance must be 100%).
- Snapshots are append-only per period. Never overwrite a locked period.

## Org model

- Roles report to roles. Streams under the GM: **Commercial** (numbers correct and on time — weekly GP, monthly net profit, STAR rating), **Operations** (billable hours, safety; supervisors signed off as capable), **Growth** (retain existing clients, win new ones). Call this COGS.
- Levels: gm → manager → supervisor → staff. Supervisor and above are `full` access; staff are `readonly` (see own role's pillars, KPI status, outstanding items as a checklist).
- Three-tier P&L: Operations judged on operational EBITDA (excludes management salaries); GM on controllable cash net profit (before depreciation, interest, allocations, one-offs); owner/board on full statutory P&L.

## Rollout sequence (the provisioning flow must follow this order)

1. Question Zero — why bring SPEC in now; what was the number or moment.
2. Organisational diagnostic — what the business does well today (evidence), what it wants to do well in years 1–3, same thing or different.
3. Financial Truth Matrix — business functioning? financials functioning? numbers bad vs reporting bad?
4. First Meeting — what success looks like, commercial reality, cost/revenue coverage checklist, realistic timeline (results 3–12 months, minimum term 6), confidence, trust, communication, systems in use, NPS baseline, agreements, early-warning signs, success/failure definition.
5. Org chart as roles → COGS streams → assign people.
6. KPIs — two per pillar per role, negotiated. Early-warning levers in order: billables/GP first, invoicing variance (1%) second, sector-segmented conversion third.
7. Rhythm — weekly SOG/COGS meeting (solutions-focused), daily notes, monthly period lock + board output.

## Monthly board output (generated by Claude)

Each pillar vs 90% with one headline driver; gate status; plain-terms section (going well / needs attention / what we need from the owner); data-integrity flags; never present an empty template as a result.

## Stack and conventions

Next.js App Router + TypeScript, Drizzle ORM (SQLite in dev, Supabase Postgres in prod, RLS by `tenant_id`), Tailwind, Anthropic API, Vercel. Scores are computed, never stored. Pure logic in `src/lib/`, tested in `tests/` with vitest. Small commits, one page or one function per session. Australian English in UI copy.

## Seed data

`seed/diagnostic.json` — Question Zero, organisational diagnostic, First Meeting questions. `seed/criteria_templates.json` — generic role criteria per pillar by stream. `seed/rulebook.json` — pattern → action rules from prior engagements (anonymised).
