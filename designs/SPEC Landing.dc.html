The deployment journey

From signing up to being SPEC

0 Sign in online

Name the business. Nothing else.

A work email, the business name, sector and size band. The signer becomes the top role. SPEC is delivered through Claude, so registering Claude is the first gate — the journey opens once it is in place.

1 Week 1

Roles first, people second

Claude proposes the roles the business needs and two KPIs per pillar for each one, tuned to your sector. Targets are negotiated, not imposed — the app records proposed against agreed. Then people go into roles.

2 Weeks 1–2

The cascade

Each manager repeats the same steps for their own reports. They see their own scorecard first — it scores the role's expectations, not the person. Staff get the read-only checklist view.

3 Month 1 onward

Run the rhythm

Weekly meeting logged, month scored, period locked. Claude generates the board output from the data — pillars against 90%, gates, what is needed from the owner, and a flag on anything the data cannot support.

Hard gates

Two things that never average out

Zero Harm and Clear to Work are pass or fail, reported separately from every score. No partial credit, and no month where a good average hides an expired ticket or an injury.
