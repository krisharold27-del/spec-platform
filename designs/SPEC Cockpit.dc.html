Domain: spec.com

Future acquisition

Currently on specbizhq.com. spec.com is a premium single-word .com already registered by a third party — not available to buy outright from a registrar. To get it you buy it FROM whoever holds it, via a broker (e.g. GoDaddy Domain Broker, Sedo, Squadhelp) who contacts the owner and negotiates on your behalf.

Cost is whatever the owner will accept, not a listed price — for a short, dictionary-word .com typically anywhere from the low tens of thousands to well into six figures USD, sometimes more if it's actively used or brandable. Broker fee is usually 10–20% of the sale, paid by the buyer.

Realistic move now: keep specbizhq.com, revisit spec.com once revenue justifies a serious offer — a broker can quietly check the owner's appetite without tipping your hand.
